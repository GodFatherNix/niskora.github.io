Questions

If yours is not here, send it through the contact form.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope. After the discovery call we send a written fixed-price proposal before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

A standard business website usually takes three to five weeks. Larger systems are scheduled in the proposal.

Do you work with clients outside Chennai?

Yes. We work remotely across India and with international clients, with video calls and shared progress updates.

Who owns the work after delivery?

You do. Designs, code and documentation are handed over to you at the end of the project.
